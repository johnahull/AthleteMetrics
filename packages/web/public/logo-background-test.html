✅ Why Transparent Background is Perfect

  • Adapts to any background: Looks great on light, dark, or colorful wallpapers
  • Respects user preferences: Works with system dark mode
  • iOS handles it automatically: Apple adds their own background styling
  • Android flexibility: Modern launchers add appropriate backgrounds
  • Professional appearance: Matches native app icons

❌ Why Adding White Background Would Be Bad

  • Looks bad on dark wallpapers: Bright white square stands out awkwardly
  • Double-container on iOS: White square inside iOS's container
  • Ignores dark mode: Doesn't respect user's system preferences
  • Less flexible: Can't adapt to future design trends

📋 Verdict

Your current transparent logo is perfect! No background needed. ✨
